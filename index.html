About Kaka Humanitarian Foundation

Our Mission

To restore dignity and transform lives by providing essential life sustaining support which including food, water, shelter, education, and healthcare, that means fostering empowerment within our community which break the cycle of poverty

Our Vision

A world where no one goes hungry, every child receives a quality education, the sick and imprisoned are visited with hope, and the vulnerable are empowered to live meaningful, dignified lives

What We Do

Feeding the Hungry (SDG 2: Zero Hunger)

Providing nutritious meals to individuals and families experiencing food insecurity to ensure no one in our community goes to bed hungry.

Empowerment Through Education (SDG 4: Quality Education)

Promoting education as a primary means to empower individuals and break the cycle of poverty, ensuring inclusive and equitable learning opportunities for all.

Clean Water Access (SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation)

Giving drink to the thirsty by ensuring reliable access to clean and safe drinking water for vulnerable populations.

Healthcare Support & Comfort (SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being)

Visiting and caring for the sick by providing essential healthcare support, medical aid, and offering comfort to those in pain.

Restoring Dignity & Transforming Lives (SDG 1: No Poverty)

Providing essential life-sustaining support to the vulnerable and less privileged to restore their dignity and foster long-term empowerment.

Shelter for the Homeless (SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities)

Creating safe spaces and supporting transitional housing initiatives to ensure that the homeless have a secure place to stay.

Justice and Advocacy (SDG 16: Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions)

Advocating for a more just and compassionate society where the less privileged are not forgotten, and visiting the imprisoned to offer support and hope.

Clothing the Needy (SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production)

Distributing clothing and other essential items to those in need, ensuring resources reach the most vulnerable.
